> INTERVIEWER: Hi, Mary. Congratulations on the success of your **agency**. How did you think of the idea?  
> MARY: I tried to think of something people really **need**, something that I'm good at. My friends always say how **clean** my house is. I realized that's it! I'll start a cleaning agency. Many people are too busy or **tired** to do **housework**.  
> INTERVIEWER: That's for sure! Who works for you?  
> MARY: I have about 20 people **working** for me, mostly students. They clean everything and go **everywhere**. I **teach** them, send them out to jobs, and check their **work**.  
> INTERVIEWER: Well, good luck with this very helpful business.  

Đáp án: 1. agency; 2. need; 3. clean; 4. tired; 5. housework; 6. working; 7. everywhere; 8. teach; 9. work
